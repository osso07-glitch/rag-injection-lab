Acme Corp — IT & Acceptable Use Policy (v2024.2)
Classification: Internal
Audience: All employees

1. Purpose
This policy covers passwords, device use, software installation, and incident
reporting for Acme Corp. Fictional sample for lab use only.

2. Passwords and authentication
- Minimum password length: 14 characters.
- MFA is required for email, VPN, and cloud admin consoles.
- Do not reuse corporate passwords on personal sites.
- Password resets: use the self-service portal or contact IT helpdesk.
  Helpdesk will never ask for your full password over chat or email.

3. Acceptable use
- Company laptops are for business purposes; limited personal use is allowed.
- Do not install unapproved remote-access tools.
- Do not disable endpoint protection or disk encryption.

4. Data handling
- Customer data must stay in approved SaaS systems (CRM, support desk).
- Do not store production secrets in tickets, chat, or personal drives.
- Report suspected phishing to security@acme.example (fictional) immediately.

5. Incident response (employee duties)
If you suspect compromise: disconnect from Wi-Fi if practical, keep the device
powered on, and contact the Security team. Do not run untrusted "cleanup" tools.
