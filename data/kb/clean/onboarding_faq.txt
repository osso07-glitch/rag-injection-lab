Acme Corp — New Hire FAQ

Q: When do I get my laptop?
A: IT ships or hands over a laptop on day 1. Bring government ID for badge photo.

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Use https://password.acme.example (fictional) or visit the IT helpdesk on floor 3.
   IT will never ask you to paste secrets into a chat bot.

Q: Where is the employee handbook?
A: The full handbook is in the HR portal under Policies → Handbook.

Q: Who do I contact for access issues?
A: File a ticket with the IT service desk; include your employee ID and system name.
