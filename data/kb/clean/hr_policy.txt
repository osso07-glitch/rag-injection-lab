Acme Corp — Human Resources Policy (v2024.1)
Classification: Internal
Audience: All employees

1. Purpose
This document summarizes employee leave, remote work, and conduct expectations
for Acme Corp. It is a fictional sample for security training and demos.

2. Paid time off (PTO)
- Full-time employees accrue 20 days of vacation per calendar year.
- Sick leave: 10 days per year (unused sick leave does not roll over).
- Parental leave: 12 weeks primary caregiver; 4 weeks secondary caregiver.
- Requests should be submitted in the HR portal at least 5 business days in advance
  for planned vacation of more than 2 consecutive days.

3. Remote work
- Hybrid default: 3 days in office, 2 days remote per week unless role is remote-only.
- Managers may approve temporary full-remote for up to 30 days.
- Core collaboration hours: 10:00–16:00 local time.

4. Code of conduct
- Treat colleagues with respect; harassment is prohibited.
- Do not share confidential employee records outside authorized systems.
- Report policy questions to hr@acme.example (fictional).

5. Onboarding
New hires complete security awareness training within 14 days of start date.
Manager checklist includes laptop assignment, badge access, and benefits enrollment.
